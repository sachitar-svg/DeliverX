📖 How it works

🏠 Direct Delivery

  1. Select order and set type to Direct
  2. Click Generate OTP — OTP appears on your screen
  3. OTP is also sent to customer via SMS 📱
  4. Ask customer: "What OTP did you receive?"
  5. Enter the OTP they tell you
  6. Capture GPS and click Verify & Confirm Delivery

📦 Hub Delivery

  1. Select order and set type to Hub
  2. Click Generate OTP to get OTP-1
  3. Travel to micro-hub with the parcel 🚚
  4. Show OTP-1 to the hub manager
  5. Hub manager verifies and sends OTP-2 to customer
  6. Click Verify & Confirm Delivery to complete handoff
